Research and projects

AI-Driven BLAS Library

Research project (TER), Paris-Saclay
With Rosemarie Streefkerk, supervised by Oguz Kaya
Graded 19/20

The question was whether a single-precision BLAS library, written entirely by a code model under natural-language prompting, can match hand-optimised references on AVX2 and OpenMP hardware. Nineteen kernels across all three levels, benchmarked against OpenBLAS. Rosemarie Streefkerk and I developed every part together, refining each topic over several rounds. The answer is yes, on one condition: the human does the hardware diagnosis. Across 36 tasks the model failed 8 times, and almost every failure was mathematically correct code that was pathological on real silicon: column-stride access, false sharing, a sequential reduction where a binary tree was needed. None of them were algorithmic errors, and the model could not diagnose any of them unprompted.

Autotuning the SGEMM block sizes with Bayesian optimisation reached the best result in the fewest trials, 66.92 GF/s in 25 evaluations, and every search strategy beat the default configuration by 10 to 15 percent.
